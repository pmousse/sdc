The Gap

Most clothes don't last because they were never meant to

Fast fashion trains us to replace rather than repair. A loose button, a frayed seam, a hem that has ridden up. Small things that send perfectly good fabric to a drawer. We believe clothing deserves better than that. Every garment has a shape waiting to be restored, a fit waiting to be found.

What We Do

Three ways we work with cloth

01

Bespoke Creation

From sketch to fitting to final stitch. We design and construct garments from scratch: dresses, suits, coats, shaped entirely to your measurements and your life. Two to four fittings, depending on complexity.

02

Alteration & Fit

Off-the-rack rarely means off-the-line. We take what you already own and make it move with your body: waistlines taken in, shoulders adjusted, lengths perfected. The best-fitting piece in your closet might already be there.

03

Mending & Restoration

Visible mending, invisible repair, full restoration. A torn heirloom, a worn elbow, a broken zipper. We treat every garment with the same care, making the repair part of the story rather than hiding it.

Philosophy

Slow work, worn every day

We don't rush. A garment takes as long as it takes: pattern drafting, muslin fittings, hand-basting, final construction. The result is something you will reach for year after year, because it was made for the person you are, not a mannequin in a warehouse.

Every repair, every alteration, every new piece carries the same promise: it will be done well, and it will last.

How It Works

From first conversation to final fitting

01

Consultation

Bring an idea, a photo, or an existing garment. We discuss the shape, the fabric, the occasion, and whether you are looking for something new or a restoration.

02

Pattern & Muslin

We draft a custom pattern from your measurements and build a test version in plain cotton. This muslin fit is where the real shaping happens.

03

Construction

Cut the final fabric, baste by hand, stitch on the machine, finish the edges. Hand-finished details where they matter: buttonholes, hems, linings.
